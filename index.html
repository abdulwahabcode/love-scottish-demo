Our Story

Where a coffee lounge
meets a gift boutique

Love Scottish began with a simple idea: a place in the heart of Hawick where the coffee is taken as seriously as the welcome. Step inside and you'll find warm walnut finishes, the gentle hum of the espresso machine, and shelves of hand-picked Scottish gifts waiting to be discovered.

Our window changes with the seasons — spring blooms, summer brights, autumn harvests and a full festive display each winter — so there's always something new to linger over with your flat white.

  • Specialty coffee, brewed with care
  • Curated Scottish gifts & homeware
  • Fresh seasonal window displays
  • Warm, unhurried, welcoming — always
